# Marbling Tray (`marbling-tray`)

**Metaphor:** a bookbinder's marbling trough (raw colour drops on cream size, a pinned comb, a sheet laid once) → diffusion image generation: start from random noise, every comb pass (denoising step) organises it, the laid sheet is the final image.
**Series:** how-ai-works · ai-tools
**Axes:** full-colour · serif · 3D · physical-object

## Look
- **Palette:** `#D8A23A` ochre page · `#2B2321` umber ink, walnut · `#EFE6D2` cream size, tabs · `#7A2430` oxblood highlight, drops · `#233A6B` indigo drops, caption pill
- **Fonts (Google Fonts):** Cormorant Garamond 700 + italic (title, values, lining figures) / Spectral 500 (caption, tab labels) / DM Mono 400-500 (meta, tab index)
- **Grid / layout:** margins 90. Meta row y 298–334 (series · No. ep left, date right). Title y 350–~630, fitted 124→72 px. Trough y ~630–1150 (CSS 3D, rotateX 49°, perspective 1750), scaled to fit between title and tabs. Swatch tabs y ~1130–1410 pinned to the rim, x 90–900. Caption y ~1430–1560. Source y 1584.
- **Signature elements:**
  1. A real marbling surface computed from a hard-coded drop list (ink-drop + tine-line displacement): back half = raw drops (noise), front half = combed nonpareil rows (denoised).
  2. A walnut comb with brass caps and steel pins standing on the split line: the denoising step made physical.
  3. Swatch tabs pinned to the front rim. Each is a crop of the same tray, sampled from back (noise) to front (order), so the items read as steps. `hl` is underlined with a strip of the combed paper.

## 2-second hook
- **Frame 0 shows:** the trough split in two. Raw drops behind the comb, a finished comb pattern in front, the comb resting on the line.
- **Motion (0–2 s):** at 0.3 s the comb drags toward the back (1.4 s, power2.inOut). The marbling maths re-runs per frame with the comb line advancing, so the drops stretch into rows under the pins. On-screen text: "Dari acak jadi gambar." (4 words).
- **Why it stops the scroll:** a tactile, satisfying pull: chaos visibly becomes order in one stroke.
- **Hook type (docs/bank-hook-ending.md):** #6 Sebelum vs sesudah terbelah

## Loop ending
- **Last 1 s:** the CTA lands at ~85%. Then a blank laid sheet drops onto the size and peels away upward, taking the print with it. Fresh drops fall on the exact frame-0 spots (15 ms stagger). The comb glides from the front rim back to the mid line, combing the front half again.
- **Seam:** B, frame kembali persis. The final composition is pixel-identical to frame 0 (split tray, comb on the line). The comb's last half-pass is the same motion the hook continues.

## Caption / voice-over
- **Slot:** `data-slot="caption"` at x 90–900, y ~1430–1560 (bottom-anchored above the source line), Spectral 500, 44–46 px, 1–2 lines, no lonely last word. Active word: an indigo pill (cream text, rounded like a floating drop). It is `hl` when `hl` is in the caption, otherwise the first word.

## Notes for the video build
- Per beat, one comb pass = one step: the comb advances, then a swatch tab drops onto the rim on the word that names it and its value counts up. Camera stays still. One emphasis move per scene at most: a slow push-in on the comb line at the payoff, then hold ≥ 1.5 s.
- Risk: re-running the displacement for ~100k vertices every frame is heavy. Precompute states at the comb positions and keep the drop list identical so the loop matches. Text never sits on the marbling.
