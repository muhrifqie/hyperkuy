# Motion vocabulary

A shared vocabulary for the `signature`, hook and loop notes in specs. Compiled from NullMotion's motion template catalogue (read-only reference, `C:\BOT\NullMotion\data\template-catalog.json`), HyperFrames' animation skills, and `docs/HOUSE-RULES.md`. Use the name, then say what it does to YOUR object.

## Entrances (content arrives)
- **Rise on arcs** — items rise from below on shallow arcs and settle at equal visual weight (no bob, no bounce on text).
- **Stagger build** — a list/grid builds one element at a time, 60–120 ms apart, top-down or in reading order.
- **Mask lift** — text lines rise out of a clipping mask, words staggered.
- **Draw-in** — strokes (axes, rules, connectors, contours, outlines) draw themselves via stroke-dashoffset, then fills follow.
- **Type-on** — characters type with a blinking caret; ideal for prompts, terminals, forms.
- **Morph** — a small object (button, chip, tile) morphs width/height/radius into a large panel; its label fades as content appears.
- **Blur reveal** — an element arrives heavily blurred and sharpens as a light sweep passes (use once, not as a habit).
- **Tilt settle** — a 3D-angled object (phone, card, sheet) rotates in and settles upright with a soft glint.
- **Grow from baseline** — bars/columns/extrusions grow from zero to value; values count up and stop ≥ 1 s before the scene ends.

## Emphasis (one per beat)
- **Cursor press** — a cursor glides to the target and presses; a focus ring or selection box answers.
- **Glint sweep** — one specular sweep across a surface to mark "this is the thing".
- **Pill lock** — the key word drops into a dimensional pill while side words arrive on opposing shallow arcs (kinetic headline).
- **Stamp / slam** — a stamp, postmark or label hits the target (fast in, tiny overshoot on the stamp only, ink settles).
- **Count & lock** — digits scramble then lock to the value.
- **Highlight stroke** — a highlighter or underline wipes across the spoken word.

## Transitions (between beats)
- **Selection jump** — the selection box FLIPs from the old object to the new one.
- **Sheet slide** — the whole sheet/board scrolls one "page"; background texture moves with it.
- **Lights out** — fast dark fade (light → dark); **circle open** — circular wipe from a point (dark → light).
- **Match cut** — an element's shape becomes the next scene's element (circle → dial → planet).

## Camera (punctuation only — see HOUSE-RULES)
Default still. Allowed: one punch-in to the detail being spoken per scene, a pull-back reveal of scale, or a travel between places. Hold ≥ 1.5 s after a big move. Never zoom in and straight back out.

## Loop seams (pick one, see docs/bank-hook-ending.md in HyperFrames for the full bank)
Sentence the opening completes · same composition returns · hook question asked again · object taken apart back to its start · motion direction continues off-frame and re-enters at frame 0 · cursor back in an empty input · progress resets · music/SFX beat match.
