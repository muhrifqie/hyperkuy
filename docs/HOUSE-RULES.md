# House rules

The taste behind the catalog. Templates are judged against these before they are added.

## Look
1. **Every frame has an object.** Cards, UI, tables, diagrams, tools, maps, paper. Never text alone on an empty background.
2. Light and dark are both fine; **empty** is not.
3. **Green is never the background or dominant colour** (small functional accents like a terminal prompt are fine). Blue is the safe accent.
4. **Not childish, not tacky, not dated:** no neon glow overload, rainbow gradients, chrome/WordArt text, cheap glitch, emoji spam, glowing-brain/robot clichés, generic sci-fi HUDs. Emoji only as small accents.
5. **Editorial, concept-led design beats generic corporate.** A real-world world (contact sheet, contour map, assembly manual…) used as a metaphor for one AI idea.
6. Readable on a phone: the text sizes and safe zone in [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md).

## Motion (for the full video)
- **Liveliness comes from content, not the camera.** Typing, a cursor click into a selection box, a highlighter stroke, numbers counting, cards flipping, lists building, lines drawing themselves, split/merge, bars filling, a background change.
- **The camera is punctuation.** Default still. Move only to change idea/place, point at one detail being spoken, or reveal scale. At most 1 emphasis move per scene, ≤ 10 camera moves per minute, camera moving ≤ 50% of the runtime, hold ≥ 1.5 s after a big move, never zoom in and straight back out. Save the spectacular move for 1–2 moments per video.
- Smooth easing; text never bounces; nothing wobbles while idle. Elements appear on the word that names them.

## Hook & loop
- **Hook (0–1.5 s):** the hook object is already visible in frame 0 (it is the thumbnail), ≤ 7 words of on-screen text, no greeting or logo intro. Pay off the hook before 70% of the runtime.
- Useful hook types: the AI visibly getting it wrong · an impossible number · result first, method later · a myth crossed out · "guess first" · before/after split · "what the AI actually sees" · a relatable notification · a mysterious detail revealed · a numbered list with empty slots · a sentence left hanging. Don't repeat the same hook type twice in a row.
- **Loop ending:** put the call-to-action before the loop point (~80–90%), then make the last 0.5–1.5 s flow back into the first frame or first sentence: a sentence the opening completes, the same composition returning, the hook question asked again, the object taken apart back to its start, the cursor back in an empty input. No fade to black, no "thanks for watching".

## Facts
Numbers and claims carry a source and a date on screen. Illustrative values are labelled as illustrations.
